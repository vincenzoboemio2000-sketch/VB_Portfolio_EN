Case Study
E. Marinella
Luxury Fashion | Tailoring since 1914 | Email Marketing & CRM
Scope: Email Marketing & CRM | Klaviyo
The Client

E. Marinella is a Neapolitan maison founded in 1914, now in its fourth generation. Six mono-brand stores worldwide and international luxury distribution. Iconic product: the handcrafted tie, worn by heads of state, entrepreneurs and collectors.

The Challenge

The brand had a fragmented customer database split between Brevo and Shopify Email, no active automated flows and no structured email marketing strategy. The goal was to turn email into a recurring revenue channel, increase the repurchase rate and build a communication consistent with the brand's luxury positioning.

Activities
  • Full database migration to Klaviyo and profile unification from multiple sources
  • Design and implementation of automated flows: Welcome, Abandoned Cart, Abandoned Checkout, Cross-Sell, Winback
  • Advanced segmentation by purchase behavior, geolocation and language (ITA + ENG)
  • Creation of bilingual campaigns with luxury tone of voice: ties, foulards, women's accessories, exclusive events
  • Custom email design consistent with the brand identity: navy/gold palette, serif fonts, minimalist magazine-style layout
  • Geolocalized mailings for events (Capri store opening: 20K ITA profiles + 1.5K international profiles)

Case Study
Fusco Boutique
Multibrand Fashion | E-commerce Shopify | Full-Funnel Performance
Managed Budget: ~8.000 €/month | Google Ads + Meta Ads + Klaviyo
The Client

Fusco Boutique is a multibrand fashion retailer operating on Shopify, with a catalog of premium and luxury brands (Premiata, Golden Goose, Lemaire, Isabel Marant, Maison Margiela). Primary market Italy, with active expansion into the Netherlands, France and Germany.

The Challenge

At takeover (Q4 2024) the brand had a MER of 2.7x and a high cost per order. Advertising efficiency was insufficient and the business was operating at a loss. The goal was to scale revenue while maintaining efficiency and bring the business to profitability.

Activities
  • Full restructuring of Google Ads campaigns: 4-tier PMax (Hero, Mid, Volume, Zombie) with margin-based segmentation
  • Repricing strategy based on a 50% target margin to feed the Shopping feed with profitable products
  • Meta Ads campaign management: prospecting + retargeting with product-centered creatives
  • International expansion: EU market launch (Netherlands, France) with dedicated PMax campaigns
  • Klaviyo setup and management: automated flows + campaigns, database from 24K to 35K profiles
  • Meta lead generation for e-commerce database growth
  • Weekly performance analysis and budget reallocation based on results

Digital Acceleration
Artemisia Cornici
Home Decor | Amazon IT | Marketplace Strategy
Managed Budget: ~1.300 €/month | Amazon Ads
The Client

Artemisia Cornici is an Italian brand of handcrafted Made in Italy picture frames. Flagship products: Cuoricino and A4 format frames, positioned in the premium segment of the Amazon marketplace.

The Challenge

The brand had no digital presence whatsoever. Zero online sales, zero positioning, zero infrastructure. The goal was to build the entire commercial ecosystem on Amazon from scratch: from the catalog to the campaigns, through to generating the first sales and building a sustainable organic positioning.

Activities
  • Writing and optimization of Amazon product listings (titles, bullet points, backend keywords, A+ Content)
  • Full management of the Seller Central account and product catalog
  • Setup and management of Amazon Ads campaigns (Sponsored Products)
  • Implementation of the "Made in Italy" badge to strengthen premium positioning
  • Keyword analysis and progressive optimization for organic ranking growth
  • FBA + Prime testing to improve visibility and conversion rate
Growth in the First 7 Months

Starting from zero, the brand achieved consistent month-over-month growth in both orders and revenue. The work on product listings and keyword optimization led to the building of an organic positioning that now generates sales even without advertising investment.

New Project
Hello Bagno
Bathroom & Garden Furnishing | E-commerce | Full-Funnel Strategy
Managed Budget: ~5.000 €/month | Google Ads + Meta Ads + Klaviyo
The Client

Hello Bagno is an Italian e-commerce specialized in bathroom furnishing, with a catalog of over 20,000 SKUs and brands such as Geberit, Grohe, Ideal Standard, Paffoni. 2024 revenue: €242K, with the goal of reaching €600K.

The Challenge

The brand had a structural tracking issue (2-3 months of lost data and approximately €7,750 of budget spent without measurement), no catalog segmentation strategy by margin, no non-brand Search campaigns, and a dominant competitor (Deghi) with significantly higher brand awareness.

Activities
  • Full audit of existing campaigns and identification of 4 loss-making campaigns to pause
  • Strategy definition: focus on the Bathroom category (6 subcategories), exclusion of low-margin categories
  • Google Ads campaign restructuring: Search, Shopping, PMax, Display, YouTube and retargeting
  • Meta Ads campaign setup (Facebook + Instagram) full funnel
  • Klaviyo configuration and migration from MailChimp
  • Tracking setup correction and data infrastructure fix

Ongoing Project

Streetwear | Brand co-founded by Guè | Branding & Growth
The Client

5tate of Mind is an Italian streetwear brand with approximately 15 years of heritage in Italian hip-hop culture, co-founded by rapper Guè (Cosimo Fini). E-commerce on Shopify, current revenue approximately €250K, flat for two years.

The Challenge

Build an autonomous and scalable brand identity, reducing dependence on the artist's fanbase. The goal is to double revenue year over year to reach €1M in two years, combining branding, acquisition and retention through an omnichannel strategy.

Planned Activities
  • Integrated strategy: Meta Ads (prospecting + retargeting), Google Ads (brand protection), Klaviyo (email + WhatsApp)

  • Lead generation for Klaviyo database growth
